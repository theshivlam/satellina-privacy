Privacy Policy

Satellina · Effective date: October 22, 2026

This policy explains what information is handled when you play Satellina (the "App"), made by Shivlam ("we"). The short version: we do not ask for or sell any personal information. The App uses two services from Google: AdMob (ads) and Firebase Analytics (anonymous usage statistics). Purchases go through Google Play or the App Store. These handle some device information as described below.

Information we ask for

None. There are no accounts. We do not ask for your name, email, contacts or location.

Information stored on your device

Your game progress (cleared levels, best times, scores and medals, Daily Orbit results and streak), your settings (colours, sound, music, vibration, drag speed, assist marks, reduce motion, graphics) and simple ad timing are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the App deletes them. When you share a Daily Orbit result, the App only hands a short text (day number, time, medal and streak) to your device's share sheet; you choose where it goes.

Usage statistics (Google Firebase Analytics)

To find levels that are too hard and improve the game, the App sends anonymous gameplay events to Firebase Analytics: when practice starts and ends, when a level starts, is cleared (with the level, time and medal), is failed or skipped, when the slower-sky help is used, when a Daily Orbit or a Constellation Run is completed, when a Daily result is shared, when an ad is shown and when a purchase is made. Firebase also collects device information (model, operating system, app version, country derived from the IP address) and an app instance identifier, and may use your device's advertising identifier where you allow it. These events are not linked to your name or contact details. See the Firebase privacy information.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free and shows ads provided by Google AdMob. To show ads, measure them, limit how often you see the same ad and prevent fraud, Google may collect and use:

  • your device's advertising identifier (Android Advertising ID or Apple IDFA), when permitted;
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it;
  • device and app information, such as device model, operating system and app version;
  • information about your interaction with ads.

Ads are personalized only where you allow it:

  • In the European Economic Area, the UK and other regions where it is required, the App asks for your consent through Google's consent form before personalized ads are shown. You can change your choice at any time in the App under Settings › Ad privacy choices.
  • On iPhone, the App asks for permission to track through Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. If you decline, your IDFA is not used. You can change this in iOS Settings › Privacy & Security › Tracking.
  • On Android you can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings › Google › Ads or Settings › Privacy › Ads, depending on your device).

Some ads are optional "rewarded" ads: you choose to watch one to keep a Daily Orbit streak or to get a streak freeze. They are never required to play.

Google's use of this information is described in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services and the Google Privacy Policy.

In-app purchase (Remove Ads)

The one purchase, Remove Ads, is processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play); we never see your payment details. The App asks the store which products you own so it can restore Remove Ads, for example after a reinstall. No other service is involved.

We do not sell your data

We do not sell personal information, and we do not share it except with the services above so the App can show ads and count usage.

Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Network access

The game can be played offline. The App only uses the internet to load ads and the consent form, send anonymous usage statistics and process purchases.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date.
